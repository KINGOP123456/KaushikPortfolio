01 / About

What this study explores

The survey focuses on how college students understand and manage personal data, passwords, permissions, phishing, social-media privacy and other digital-safety practices.

Objective

To understand the level of data-privacy awareness among college students and identify common online privacy and security practices.

Methodology

Primary data was collected using a Google Form survey. The charts below are generated directly from the submitted response spreadsheet.
